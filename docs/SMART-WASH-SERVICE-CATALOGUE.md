The admin Services tab has an explicit **Add verified signboard services** action for these nine selected starter items. It adds only missing names and does not overwrite existing edits. These are starting catalogue values, not locked prices: every service can be renamed, repriced, hidden/unhidden or deleted from the same admin tab. Hidden services remain visible to staff for management and are excluded from customer booking.

## Discount

The sign advertises a Full Wash discount on Mondays and Thursdays for students and pensioners. The discount amount is not legible (only P is visible). Do not activate or advertise a discount until the owner confirms the amount, eligibility and terms.

## Publishing rules

- The nine clearly legible prices can be added through Admin → Services → **Add verified signboard services** after the dedicated Smart Wash Firebase project is configured.
- The four uncertain prices must remain unpublished until confirmed.
- Never represent the P80 carpet-cleaning starting price as a guaranteed final charge; the service name retains “From”.
- A booking or queue entry is a request. Staff confirm availability and the final transaction amount.
